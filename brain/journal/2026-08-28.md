## [Nepal and climate security] Scientists point to collapsed glacier as cause of deadly floods
Preliminary investigations cited by the BBC point to a **collapsed glacier** as the likely trigger for the Nepal–Tibet floods. This is a significant update to yesterday’s initial report about people missing: it moves the story from the immediate disaster response toward a possible physical mechanism connected with rapidly melting Himalayan ice. It remains a preliminary scientific assessment, not a final attribution. The practical implications are better glacial-lake monitoring, early-warning systems, cross-border data sharing and evacuation routes for mountain communities. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cly464k252yo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Bosnia and war-crimes accountability] Convicted war criminal Ratko Mladić dies aged 84
[[Ratko Mladić]], the former Bosnian Serb commander convicted of genocide and war crimes, has died aged **84**, according to the BBC. The International Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia sentenced him to life imprisonment in 2017 for crimes committed during the 1992–95 wars in the former [[Yugoslavia]]. His death does not alter the judgment or the historical record; it closes the life of one of the conflict’s principal convicted figures while questions about remembrance, denial and accountability remain relevant in the Western Balkans. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5ywxpryj95o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [United States and military AI] Judge rules Trump administration illegally retaliated against Anthropic
A US judge ruled that the [[Donald Trump]] administration **illegally retaliated** against [[Anthropic]], an AI company that had clashed with the [[Pentagon]] over military use of AI, the BBC reports. The ruling is a judicial finding in this case, not a general conclusion that every government action concerning the company was unlawful. The core policy issue is whether suppliers can set safety boundaries for military applications without being punished by the state, and how procurement rules should handle disagreements over model capabilities and use. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm2q7z5mlrmo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Aviation and cyber security] Hackers access data of about 8.7 million airport customers
Hackers accessed data belonging to about **8.7 million airport customers** and demanded a ransom, according to the BBC. The available report summary does not establish which data fields were exposed, whether all affected customers have been notified or whether the ransom was paid. Travellers should be cautious about follow-up phishing messages that use flight or airport details, avoid clicking unsolicited “refund” or “booking” links and contact the airport or airline through an independently verified website. For operators, the incident highlights the need to segment customer databases, protect identity data and maintain tested incident-response plans. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c7v4353rry7o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [France and energy resilience] Jellyfish force partial shutdown of Gravelines nuclear plant
Jellyfish forced a partial shutdown of France’s **Gravelines** nuclear power station for the second consecutive year, the BBC reports. The episode is a concrete example of how biological material and warmer coastal conditions can affect cooling-water intakes, even when the underlying reactor technology is operating as designed. The report does not establish the duration of the outage or its effect on national electricity supply. Nuclear operators and regulators need monitoring, intake protection and contingency planning for recurring marine-environment disruptions. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cy8znx3g4e2o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [United States and Russia] CIA director makes secret return trip to Moscow
CIA director [[John Ratcliffe]] made a secret **10,000-mile** return trip to [[Moscow]], a development the BBC describes as non-routine. The available report does not disclose the full agenda or establish that the visit produced an agreement. A senior intelligence-channel trip can nevertheless indicate an attempt to preserve communication, test possible diplomatic openings or manage risks that public diplomacy cannot address. The important watchpoints are official confirmation, subsequent US–Russia contacts and whether the journey affects negotiations or security incidents. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2z8l8xdg2o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Cyber security and artificial intelligence] Tech firms warn attacks using AI will rapidly improve
Leading technology companies warned that AI-enabled cyber-attacks could become more sophisticated within **months**, according to a letter reported by the BBC. This is an industry warning rather than a quantified forecast, so it should not be read as proof of a fixed timetable. The practical response is defensive: enforce phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication, reduce excessive account privileges, test backups, monitor unusual automation and train staff against synthetic messages. For Poland and the EU, the issue is also one of resilience in hospitals, utilities, public administration and smaller suppliers that may lack dedicated security teams. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwyz11475l1o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Social media and children] UN calls for broader reform after Meta settlement
The [[United Nations]] said reform of social media should extend beyond [[Meta]] after its child-safety settlement; California’s attorney general is also examining [[TikTok]] and [[YouTube]] for teen-safety features, the BBC reports. This is a policy and regulatory development, not evidence that the proposed features are already effective. For parents, platform labels and promises should not replace age-appropriate supervision, privacy settings and conversations about persuasive design, harmful content and contacting strangers. The wider significance is that child safety is shifting from isolated lawsuits toward common standards applied across platforms. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c17j00lnn41o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Nepal and disaster response] Flooding death toll reaches 939 as rescuers search for workers
Rescuers in [[Nepal]] used explosives to reach hydropower workers trapped by landslides, while the reported flooding death toll reached **939**, according to the BBC. The scale highlights infrastructure exposure, difficult emergency access and the human cost of weak resilience planning. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5ylvgq9q71o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [United States and defence] Army secretary resigns after months of tension
[[Dan Driscoll]], the US Army secretary, resigned after about 18 months in office, the BBC reports. This is an institutional signal rather than proof of a specific policy reversal; its significance depends on the reasons for the tension and on who replaces him. It is relevant to US force management and defence-policy continuity. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/czjz31dk1xmo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Afghanistan and migration] Six million deportees face forced returns under the Taliban
The BBC reports that six million people have been forced back to [[Afghanistan]], with another million returning from [[Pakistan]] and [[Iran]] since the start of this year. Many have little connection to the country and must rebuild their lives under the [[Taliban]]. The development increases humanitarian pressure and may drive further irregular migration. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c2e074nn8eko?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [China and India] Japanese companies shift investment towards India
Japanese companies are increasing their focus on [[India]] as domestic demographic pressures persist and risks connected with [[China]] deepen, according to the BBC. This gradual realignment illustrates how supply-chain diversification and geopolitical risk influence corporate capital allocation, with consequences for European trade and industrial strategy. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cz64955d56po?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Shein and financial markets] Shares fall in Hong Kong debut
[[Shein]] shares fell during the company’s long-awaited Hong Kong stock-market debut, the BBC reports. The listing follows years of attempts to sell shares in New York and London. Investor reception matters for expansion and for debates over Chinese-founded firms, supply-chain transparency and access to Western capital markets. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3v0qrz7z25o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Amazon and competition policy] US states allege billions in ad-pricing manipulation
The US Federal Trade Commission and states allege that [[Amazon]] rigged billions of dollars in advertising prices; Amazon says the regulator misunderstands its market, according to the BBC. The allegations are not established findings. The case could affect digital-advertising competition, platform regulation and technology-company compliance in Europe. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvgy91nvy27o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [AI and macroeconomics] Bank of England governor warns G20 about downturn risk
[[Andrew Bailey]], governor of the [[Bank of England]], warned the G20 that AI-related volatility and energy shocks linked to the US–Iran conflict could contribute to a global economic downturn, the BBC reports. This is a risk warning, not a forecast, connecting the AI investment cycle with energy costs, market expectations and geopolitical disruption. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c99dym3prl1o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

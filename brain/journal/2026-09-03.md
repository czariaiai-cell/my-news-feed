## [United Kingdom and irregular migration] Smuggling gangs switch to larger boats
A BBC investigation found that a crackdown limiting the supply of small boats is pushing previously rival Channel smuggling groups to cooperate and use “mega-dinghies”. The development may change the risk profile of crossings: fewer available craft does not necessarily mean fewer attempts, while larger vessels could increase the consequences of failure. It also illustrates how enforcement can displace rather than eliminate criminal logistics. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ce3rn93de9eo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [United States and currency] US Mint issues Trump $1 coin
A special-edition US Mint one-dollar coin bears [[Donald Trump]]’s portrait, making him the first living US president on American currency in a century, according to the BBC. The item has symbolic rather than immediate macroeconomic importance, but it demonstrates how state institutions and national symbolism are being used in the current political environment. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c7507g9v992o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Technology and critical infrastructure] Airport hack data of 8.7 million people published
Criminals have published data belonging to 8.7 million people after a cyberattack on the company operating [[Manchester Airport]], [[Stansted Airport]] and [[East Midlands Airport]], the BBC reports. The incident shows that aviation resilience includes identity-data protection as well as physical security. For European operators, the practical priorities are breach containment, notification, credential protection and tested continuity plans. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c74k39g3ee5o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Artificial intelligence governance] UK Lords call for AI “kill switch” powers
Members of the UK [[House of Lords]] are backing legal powers to halt high-risk AI systems, describing them as a safety net against runaway systems. The proposal is not yet a policy outcome, but it adds institutional pressure for emergency intervention, auditability and clear responsibility among developers and operators. European regulators will need to balance enforceability with innovation and cross-border deployment. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cn9wv80j9w9o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

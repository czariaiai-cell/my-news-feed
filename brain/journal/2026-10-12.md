## Key developments

## [Poland and domestic security] Poland detains man suspected of planning assassination of PM Tusk
Polish police detained a man after receiving credible information about possible attacks against Prime Minister [[Donald Tusk]] and Defence Minister [[Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz]]. The report describes an active security investigation, not a completed attack or a court finding. The case is significant for [[Poland]] because it combines protection of senior officials, politically motivated violence and the wider security environment created by the war in [[Ukraine]]. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6n9rvynl5p5o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Saudi Arabia and regional security] Houthi strike kills one at Riyadh airport
A second [[Houthi]] strike on [[Riyadh]] airport in two days killed one person and injured 11, according to the report. Repeated attacks on civilian aviation infrastructure raise immediate questions about air-defence performance, flight disruption and the risk that the [[Saudi Arabia]]-[[Yemen]] conflict expands into a sustained campaign against critical transport nodes. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr93znyk44e1o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Russia and financial transparency] Documents falsified to evade tax on Abramovich's wealth
A BBC investigation found backdated paperwork that allegedly concealed [[Roman Abramovich]]'s yachts, mansions and other assets from tax records. The findings concern alleged document falsification and require legal follow-up, but they illustrate how complex ownership structures and offshore documentation can obstruct sanctions, taxation and enforcement involving Russian oligarch wealth. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c64g1l5389kvo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Artificial intelligence and regulation] Microsoft president says AI is less regulated than milk
[[Microsoft]] president Brad Smith called for global rules for [[artificial intelligence]] and argued that the technology is less regulated than milk. The proposal is advocacy by a major industry executive, not a new law, but it highlights the policy gap between rapidly deployed AI systems and sector-specific safety, liability and disclosure regimes. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6rmy7zmvx3lo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Ukraine and Russian technology infrastructure] Ukraine strikes Yandex data centre in third attack this week
[[Ukraine]] targeted a [[Yandex]] data centre in what the report describes as the third attack on the Russian technology company in one week, forcing it to suspend operations. Beyond the immediate military effect, repeated strikes against a major digital provider show how the war is extending into commercial data infrastructure and may affect service continuity, redundancy and cyber-security risk in [[Russia]]. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw0k0g57mk3zo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Iran and the United States] Tehran offers to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days
Iran proposed reopening the [[Strait of Hormuz]] within seven days if the [[United States]] accepts its conditions. A US official described mediated contacts as constructive, while the precise terms remain contested. This is a new diplomatic development, not confirmation of a settlement. Hormuz is critical to global energy shipping, so any durable arrangement could affect oil prices, insurance and European inflation. ## [Ukraine and critical infrastructure] Zelensky says Russian strikes target Ukrainian data centres
President [[Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] said [[Russia]] is attacking Ukrainian data centres to disrupt connectivity, work and education. The statement describes an alleged strategic effect; independent attribution and the full damage assessment require verification. The development highlights civilian dependence on resilient cloud, telecoms and energy infrastructure in wartime. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c84gkwgk7d06o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Germany and defence] Students protest against the prospect of compulsory military service
Students demonstrated across [[Germany]] against military-service policy. A voluntary-service law took effect in January, while organisers fear that compulsory service could follow. The protests are relevant to [[Europe]] because Germany's force-generation debate affects NATO readiness, defence spending and the political sustainability of rearmament. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cxnvlnve52qdo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Morocco and domestic politics] PAM wins most seats but falls short of a parliamentary majority
Morocco's [[PAM]] won the largest share in parliamentary elections but did not secure a majority. Coalition negotiations will shape the government's economic agenda and preparations for the 2030 World Cup. This adds a North African political development with implications for regional stability and European partnership. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/26/pam-wins-big-in-morocco-parliament-elections-but-falls-short-of-majority?traffic_source=rss)

## [Technology and regulation] TikTok agrees to pay $100m in Alabama safety settlement
[[TikTok]] agreed to pay at least $100m to [[Alabama]] shortly before trial in a case concerning alleged risks to teenagers. The settlement is not a general judicial finding on every allegation, but it raises the regulatory cost of platform design and youth-safety claims in the United States.

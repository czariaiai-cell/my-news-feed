## [United States and military justice] Pentagon says Fort Hood attacker's execution will be livestreamed
The US Defence Secretary said the execution of the Fort Hood attacker by firing squad will be made public via livestream. A legal expert described the decision as unprecedented and legally uncertain. The case raises questions about execution protocols, transparency and the limits of executive action in the [[United States]]. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmy0r96xygx6o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Monaco and Ukraine] Suspect in Monaco bomb attack speaks from Ukrainian prison
Vitalii Zhykovych, accused of involvement in an attempted assassination of Ukrainian-born businessman Vadym Yermolayev in [[Monaco]], gave the BBC his account from a Ukrainian prison. The case illustrates how organised-crime and political-security investigations can cross borders and depend on cooperation between jurisdictions. The suspect's account is an allegation, not a court finding. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckgel9044vqeo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Technology and financial markets] Nvidia-backed AI data-centre firm cancels landmark listing
Firmus, an AI data-centre company backed by [[Nvidia]], abandoned a planned landmark listing, citing market volatility and prevailing market conditions. The decision is a concrete signal that enthusiasm for AI infrastructure does not remove financing risk: expensive capacity expansion remains exposed to valuation changes and tighter investor risk appetite. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ck9dzpw4ll8po?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [United States and energy] Trump targets fuel costs before the midterms
[[Donald Trump]] is seeking to reduce fuel costs ahead of the US midterm elections as petrol prices and the wider cost of living become major political issues. Lower prices may help households in the short term, but policy aimed at suppressing prices can conflict with supply constraints, energy-market incentives and fiscal costs. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cv2d6x749lwro?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Australia and intellectual property] Adidas sues White Fox over four-stripes design
[[Adidas]] is suing Australian clothing label White Fox over its use of a four-stripes design and is seeking an injunction and damages. The dispute shows how brand protection can become a material risk for fast-growing fashion companies, especially where visual identity is central to online sales. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9e8ld448km8o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

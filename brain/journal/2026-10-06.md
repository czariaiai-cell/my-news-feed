## Key developments

## [United States and United Kingdom] Trump says a threat led to bomber withdrawal from RAF Fairford
This materially updates yesterday’s report: President Donald Trump now says a threat caused the withdrawal of US B-1 bombers from RAF Fairford, while the Pentagon confirms their return to US home stations. The disclosure increases uncertainty around the security incident and raises questions for [[NATO]] force protection and allied communication. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwj3413e5m1lo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Yemen and Red Sea] Yemeni military claims the Red Sea waterway is secured
This is a new battlefield claim following yesterday’s announcement of an all-out war against the Houthis. Saudi-backed forces say they secured the waterway, but reports conflict over control of Mokha near Bab al-Mandab. Shipping, insurance and humanitarian-risk assessments should therefore not yet treat the route as stable. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw8rzd6lp0xpo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [France] Nationwide school protests expected after injuries and mass arrests
France is preparing for a national day of demonstrations supporting students demanding more investment in education, after earlier protests produced injuries and mass arrests. The episode will test the government’s handling of public-order tensions and could intensify political pressure over public services in [[Europe]]. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr4g1q1elxnjo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Canada] Separatist party projected to win Quebec election
The Parti Québécois is projected to have won a minority government in Quebec and has pledged to hold an independence referendum in the coming years. The result opens a new test for Canadian unity, with uncertainty over the timing and feasibility of a referendum likely to affect federal-provincial politics. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crly09gz7ew4o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [United States and AI] Pentagon stops using Anthropic tools after blacklisting the company
The Pentagon has stopped using Anthropic’s AI tools after labelling the company a supply-chain risk; Anthropic had refused to remove safety guardrails. The dispute makes the trade-off between military deployment, vendor control and model safeguards concrete, and may influence procurement standards across the US national-security ecosystem. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5j9x9pr0240o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

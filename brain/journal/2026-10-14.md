A Ukrainian national is due to stand trial in [[Germany]] over allegations connected to the [[Nord Stream]] pipeline explosions. The proceedings create a new legal and diplomatic phase in the long-running case, separate from yesterday’s reporting on Russian energy ceasefire diplomacy, and could affect European debate about attribution, infrastructure protection and relations with [[Ukraine]]. ## [United Kingdom and national security] MI5 chief warns of threats from Russia, Iran and AI
The head of [[MI5]] said threats from [[Russia]], [[Iran]] and [[artificial intelligence]] now sit alongside terrorism at roughly comparable operational scale. The assessment is a new intelligence and resilience signal, distinct from yesterday’s ministerial warning about a possible cyber breach, and points to a broader convergence of state threats, terrorism and AI-enabled risk. The report provides a practical infrastructure and labour-market story rather than repeating yesterday’s AI-policy coverage: staffing losses and operational disruption could affect service reliability during the company’s wider transition.

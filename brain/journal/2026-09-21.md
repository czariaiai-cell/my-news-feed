## [Germany and domestic politics] Merz calls state-election result a “disaster” but vows to stay
Exit polls reported damaging losses for Chancellor [[Friedrich Merz]]’s centre-right [[CDU]] in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern and Berlin. Merz said he would remain in office. This domestic setback matters for [[Poland]] because Berlin’s coalition stability affects European defence, migration, industrial policy and support for [[Ukraine]]. ## [Russia and Ukraine] Moscow reports its largest drone attack, with 450 drones downed
Moscow’s mayor said Russian air defences downed 450 drones during an overnight barrage and that two people died. The figures are claims by Russian authorities pending independent verification. This is new from yesterday: the previous journal did not include this Moscow attack, and the reported scale indicates continued long-range strike pressure. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c34gdjk1ne8yo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [European Union and Canada] Commission signals a possible path for Canada to become an EU associate member
A Canadian minister told the BBC that the [[United Kingdom]] should team up with [[Canada]] in a new European alliance, after Commission President Ursula von der Leyen proposed opening the door for Canada to become an associate member of the [[European Union]]. This is a political proposal, not an accession process. If developed, it could strengthen cooperation on trade, critical minerals, energy and security. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmed7p06epxjo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Artificial intelligence and markets] Nvidia chief rejects AI extinction warnings as “doomsday narratives”
[[Nvidia]] chief executive Jensen Huang rejected warnings that artificial intelligence could cause human extinction, according to the BBC. This is an industry position, not evidence resolving the safety debate. The practical issue for Europe and Poland remains balancing AI investment with testing, cyber-security and accountability. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr5ye7p13jg7o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

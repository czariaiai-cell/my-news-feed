## Key developments

## [United States and Europe] Trump threatens a diesel-export ban as Washington pressures European reserves
President [[Donald Trump]] threatened to restrict US diesel exports in an attempt to lower domestic prices before the November elections. The measure could tighten an already sensitive transatlantic fuel market: Europe depends on imports and any US restriction may increase competition for alternative supplies, raise transport costs and add inflationary pressure. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5zjzjgdeneeo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Russia and the Baltic] Putin says Russia is ready to use every weapon to protect Kaliningrad
[[Vladimir Putin]] warned Western countries that [[Russia]] is prepared to use every weapon to protect [[Kaliningrad]]. The statement raises the political temperature around the Russian exclave between Poland and Lithuania, where military signalling, access routes and NATO deterrence are closely connected. It is a reported threat, not evidence that an attack is imminent, but it increases the importance of clear allied communication and regional readiness. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqg7k7v499jjo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [United Kingdom and financial stability] Bank of England governor warns the AI boom could trigger market shocks
The Bank of England governor warned that rapid investment connected with the [[AI]] boom could produce market shocks. The risk is not limited to technology shares: concentrated financing, high expectations and dependence on a small number of companies can transmit losses through funds, lenders and household portfolios if growth assumptions change abruptly. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cv8e30enrkxyo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [AI governance] OpenAI fires workers over mishandling sensitive information
[[OpenAI]] said it fired workers investigated for sharing sensitive information with an outside AI evaluation group. The case highlights a difficult governance problem: independent safety testing can reveal serious model weaknesses, but access to confidential data and internal material requires strict controls. For organisations deploying AI, audit trails, least-privilege access and clear disclosure procedures are practical safeguards. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6y9z9r4ejzwo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Australia and online safety] Government says Steam and Roblox have significant child-safety gaps
The Australian government said platforms including [[Steam]] and [[Roblox]] have significant gaps in child safety. The issue concerns more than age checks: large gaming platforms combine messaging, user-generated content and commercial incentives, making moderation, parental controls and reporting systems central to risk management for children. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cjvgygzzy6lro?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## What it means for Poland and Europe
- Any US diesel-export restriction could raise European fuel and logistics costs, even if the final measure is narrower than the threat.
- Statements about Kaliningrad matter directly to Poland and the Baltic security environment; deterrence depends on credible coordination rather than alarmism.
- AI investment and AI-data governance are now linked: financial concentration and information-handling failures can reinforce each other.
- Child-safety rules for gaming platforms are likely to become a broader European regulatory question involving identity, privacy and parental responsibility.

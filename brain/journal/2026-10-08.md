## [Ukraine and Russia] Strike on buses in Kramatorsk kills at least 30, officials say

The reported attack marks a severe escalation in civilian risk and underscores the pressure on transport routes near the [[Ukraine]]-[[Russia]] front. ## [Persian Gulf security] Tanker hit by multiple projectiles off Qatar
A tanker was hit by multiple projectiles in the Gulf off [[Qatar]], with casualties reported by a maritime agency. The incident adds a new shipping and energy-security risk in a strategically important route and requires clarification of responsibility and the vessel's condition. ## [South Korea and Ukraine] Seoul recalls envoy over North Korean prisoner dispute
[[South Korea]] recalled its ambassador to [[Ukraine]] amid a diplomatic dispute after Kyiv disclosed that two North Korean prisoners of war had been transferred to Seoul. The move shows how the war is creating additional diplomatic friction involving [[North Korea]] and countries outside Europe. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckwy48rrz95vo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Venezuela and United States] US prosecutors add torture-conspiracy charge against Nicolás Maduro
Former Venezuelan leader Nicolás Maduro was charged in the [[United States]] with conspiracy to commit torture, in allegations concerning US citizens held in [[Venezuela]] during his time in power. The new accusation raises the legal and diplomatic stakes around the case and should be distinguished from a final court finding. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5pvgn7xvxpzo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [United States and technology] White House blocks Microsoft from international skilled-worker programme
The White House blocked [[Microsoft]] from using an international visa programme that US technology companies have long used to hire highly skilled workers from abroad. The decision places immigration, labour supply and technology competitiveness at the centre of the US tech-policy debate.

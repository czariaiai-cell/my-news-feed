## Key developments

## [United States and United Kingdom] US removes all bombers from RAF Fairford
The US Air Force has withdrawn all bombers from the British base at RAF Fairford without giving a reason. The move follows a recent security incident involving suspicious vehicles near the base. The unexplained change matters for [[NATO]] signalling and allied force posture in Europe, although the report does not establish that it represents a broader reduction in US commitments. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmwyve191dlko?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Brazil] Presidential election heads to a second round
Brazil's presidential election will go to a run-off on 25 October after right-wing Flávio Bolsonaro won the first round without securing more than half of the vote. The second round will make coalition-building and turnout decisive, with consequences for Brazil's domestic direction and its role in regional diplomacy and climate policy. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ck1l34ed592no?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [United States and AI] Trump creates a 'Super Intelligence Force' for AI policy
Donald Trump has announced a taskforce to oversee US AI policy and appointed the national intelligence chief to lead it. Centralising AI policy inside the national-security apparatus could accelerate state coordination, but it also raises questions about civilian oversight, competition policy and safeguards. The development is distinct from the earlier AI-generated-evidence court case: this concerns executive governance rather than courtroom use. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqj6jenp26zyo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Pakistan] Supporters of Imran Khan march towards Islamabad
Thousands of supporters of jailed former prime minister Imran Khan have marched towards Pakistan's capital after negotiations between authorities and his party ended without agreement. A confrontation would test the government's ability to maintain order while managing opposition mobilisation in a nuclear-armed state with a strategically important relationship with [[China]] and the [[United States]]. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmkg79z04w4zo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## [Yemen and Red Sea] Government announces an all-out war against the Houthis
Yemen's government has announced an all-out war to reclaim territory from the Houthis amid exchanges of strikes involving the Saudi-led coalition. A new escalation could worsen the humanitarian crisis and put further pressure on shipping and insurance across the [[Red Sea]], adding to existing regional security risks. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx8dzj9vpp01o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

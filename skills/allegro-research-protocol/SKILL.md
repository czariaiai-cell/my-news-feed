# Allegro Research Protocol

This skill provides a structured approach to researching products on Allegro:

1. **Search**: Search for the product on Allegro.
2. **Filter**: Apply criteria (Smart delivery, rating > 4.5).
3. **Analyze**: 
    - Calculate price per kg.
    - Check number of reviews (> 50 preferred, > 10 minimum).
    - Filter out listings with recurring negative reviews (broken/damaged products).
    - Check seller reputation.
4. **Select**: Identify the top 3 best listings based on the balance of Price/KG and Opinion quality.
5. **Report**: Return links, calculated price/kg, and justification for each selection.
